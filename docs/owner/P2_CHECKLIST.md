## Naya: tabs, rang, Visa aur registers

**Rang aur tabs**

- [ ] Menu kholein. Hazri, Labour, Visa, Companies, Tankhwah, Hisaab aur Rooms alag alag dikhen, aur dabane par sub-tabs khulen.
- [ ] Kisi screen par header ke neeche sub-tabs se aik tap mein doosri screen par jayen.
- [ ] **Menu > Settings > App ke rang**: teeno rang baari baari dabayein. Rang fauran badle aur aap usi screen par rahen.
- [ ] Doosri screens par ja kar dekhein, sab jagah wahi rang ho.
- [ ] App poori tarah band kar ke dobara kholein. Chuna hua rang wahi rahe.
- [ ] Rang badal kar PDF aur Excel nikalein. File ki headings bhi usi rang ki hon.

**Visa**

- [ ] **Visa > Visa ka paisa > Visa kharcha likhein**: mazdoor chunein, AED 3,000 save karein. Woh mazdoor list mein aaye, Baqi AED 3,000.00.
- [ ] Mazdoor par dabayein, **Har mahine ki qist** par AED 300 likhein.
- [ ] **Tankhwah** par jayen, **Visa qist lagayein** dabayein. Us mazdoor ke net se AED 300 kam ho. Post karein.
- [ ] Visa par wapas aayen: Wapas aaya AED 300.00, Baqi AED 2,700.00.
- [ ] Post wapas karne par Baqi phir AED 3,000.00 ho.

**Registers**

- [ ] Har register kholein: Hazri, Mazdoor, Visa, Company, Tankhwah, Advance, Hisaab, Room. Entries date ke neeche hon aur har entry ki poori tafseel saamne ho.
- [ ] Tareekh badlein, aur **Is mahine**, **Pichla mahina**, **Sab** dabayein. Entries aur totals badlen.
- [ ] Filter mein do mazdoor chunein, phir company. Sirf wahi entries rahen. **Saaf karein** se sab wapas aayen.

**PDF, Excel, WhatsApp** (yeh sirf phone par check ho sakta hai)

- [ ] **PDF, Excel ya WhatsApp** dabayein. Upar register ka naam, entries aur filter likhe hon.
- [ ] PDF: **Dekhein ya print** se khule, har page par company ka naam aur Safha number ho.
- [ ] Excel: **Kholein** ya **Phone mein save**. Excel ya Google Sheets mein khul kar headings upar ruki rahen, filter chalen, aur neeche Total ho.
- [ ] **WhatsApp** dabayein. WhatsApp khule, aap khud banda chunein aur bhejein. App khud kuch nahin bhejti.
- [ ] Jis phone par WhatsApp na ho wahan paigham aaye aur share sheet khule.

## 1. Purana data

- [ ] Naya APK purani app ke upar install karein, uninstall na karein. Companies, mazdoor, hazri aur Rozana hisaab sab wahi rahen.

## 2. Tankhwah ki screen

- [ ] **Menu > Tankhwah** kholein. Jis mazdoor ki hazri lagi hai, ya mahana contract hai, woh apni kamai ke saath nazar aaye. Manzoor karne ka koi button nahin.
- [ ] Company ka rate na ho tab bhi mazdoor ki kamai aaye. Baad mein **Rates** mein company ka rate likhein. Hazri register mein company bill khud aa jaye, aur mazdoor ki pay na badle.
- [ ] Jis mazdoor ka apna rate na ho us par laal likha ho, aur woh post na ho. Rate likhte hi laal hat jaye.
- [ ] Mazdoor par dabayein. **Allowance** aur **Katoti** add karein. Katoti wajah aur agreement ke baghair save na ho.
- [ ] Jis mazdoor ka advance baqi hai us par **Advance ki wapsi** likhein. Baqi advance se zyada save na ho.
- [ ] **Room charge lagayein** dabayein. Sab mazdoor list mein aayen. Jin par lagana hai un par tick karein, raqam likhein, aur **lagayein** dabayein. Sirf un ki katoti Tankhwah mein aaye. Koi note nahin poocha jata.
- [ ] Katoti kamai se zyada kar ke dekhein. Us mazdoor par laal likha aaye aur woh post na ho. Baqi post hon.

## 3. Aap ki apni misal

Aik naya mazdoor banayein, mahana tankhwah AED 1,000, aur contract ki shuruat pichle mahine se pehle ki tareekh.

- [ ] Pichle mahine par jayen aur **Tankhwah post karein**. Mazdoor ko dena hai AED 1,000.00 aaye.
- [ ] Mazdoor par dabayein, **Adaigi likhein**, AED 500 save karein. **Abhi baqi** AED 500.00 ho.
- [ ] Is mahine par aayen. **Pichla baqaya** AED 500.00, **Is mahine ka net** AED 1,000.00, **Total dena hai** AED 1,500.00 ho.
- [ ] Post karein aur kuch ada na karein. **Abhi baqi** AED 1,500.00 rahe, aur Rozana hisaab mein wahi AED 500 ki aik hi entry ho.

## 4. Galti theek karna

- [ ] Post hue mahine mein **Post wapas karein**. Wajah ke baghair na ho. Mahina phir khul jaye, aur jo paisay diye woh mazdoor ke hisaab mein rahen.
- [ ] Jis mahine ke baad agla mahina bhi post ho, us ko wapas karne par paigham aaye ke pehle agla mahina wapas karein.
- [ ] Post hui hazri ki correction karein. Agle mahine **Pichli hazri ka hisaab** mein sirf farq aaye.

## 5. Baqi screens par asar

- [ ] Room charge wala mahina post hone ke baad **Rooms** mein woh raqam **Kiraya mila** mein aa jaye.
- [ ] Mazdoor ki screen par **Tankhwah baqi** aur **Advance baqi** sahi hon, dobara kuch likhna na pare.

## 5a. Mazdoor register aur Hazri register

- [ ] **Labour > Mazdoor register** kholein. Har mazdoor ka apna hisaab ho, tareekh ke hisaab se: kis company aur site par, kitne ghante, kis rate par, kitni kamai, kitna advance, kis din kitni adaigi, aur har line ke baad **Baqi**.
- [ ] Baqi khud jor kar dekhein: kamai - advance - katoti, room aur visa ki qist - adaigi.
- [ ] Pichla mahina chunein. Pehli line **Pichla baqi** ho.
- [ ] PDF aur Excel nikalein. PDF mein sab se upar sab mazdooron ka khulasa ho. Excel mein doosri sheet par.
- [ ] **Hazri > Hazri register** ka PDF nikalein. Upar mazdoor ke hisaab se din, ghante aur pay ho, phir company aur site ke hisaab se bill, pay aur farq.
- [ ] Jis row ka company rate nahin us par **Rate nahin** likha ho.

## 5b. Group, Salary slip aur Malik ki sheet

- [ ] **Hazri > Mazdoor group** mein group banayein. Jis mazdoor ka apna rate hai, us par tick karte hi neeche **Apni tankhwah** likha aaye.
- [ ] **Badlein** dabayein, aur is group ke liye fi ghanta ya fi din ka doosra rate likhein. Neeche **Is group mein** likha aaye.
- [ ] Jis mazdoor ka apna rate nahin, us ke neeche **Group ki dehari** aaye. Hazri lagayein, aur har aik ko sahi raqam mile.
- [ ] Purani hazri dekhein: Ahmad ke 7 ghante par ab AED 70 hon, AED 10 nahin. Jo mahina post ho chuka hai woh nahin badlega.
- [ ] **Tankhwah > mazdoor > Salary slip** dabayein, phir **WhatsApp**. Slip PDF mein jaye aur mazdoor khud chunein.
- [ ] Slip mein company ka rate, bill ya nafa kahin na ho.
- [ ] Slip ka hisaab khud jor kar dekhein: pichla baqi + kaam - advance - room - visa - katoti - ada kiya = baqi.
- [ ] Din ba din wale hisse mein har din ki company, site, ghante, rate aur total hon. Neeche **Jo paise liye** tareekh ke saath hon.
- [ ] Agle mahine ki slip nikalein. Us ka pichla baqi is mahine ke baqi ke barabar ho.
- [ ] **Tankhwah > Malik ki sheet** ka PDF aur Excel nikalein. Har company ka rate, bill, mazdoor pay aur nafa ho, aur upar **Andazan nafa**.
- [ ] Excel asal Excel ya Google Sheets mein kholein. Teen sheets hon: Khulasa, Company wise, Mazdooron ka hisaab.
- [ ] Agar aap ke paas bohat mazdoor hain: Hazri register ka Excel banne mein kitna waqt laga, likh dein.

## 5c. Adaigi, zyada adaigi aur baqi tankhwah

- [ ] Do mahine ki hazri lagayein aur kuch ada na karein. **Aaj** aur **Tankhwah** par peela khana aaye: kitne mazdoor, kitni tankhwah baqi.
- [ ] Us khane ko dabayein. **Baqi tankhwah** mein har mazdoor, har mahina aur kitne din late likha ho.
- [ ] Mazdoor ko dabayein. Form mein har baqi mahina apni raqam ke saath aaye, purana mahina pehle bhara ho.
- [ ] Raqam kam karein. Mahinon ki raqam khud badle, purane mahine se.
- [ ] Sirf naye mahine mein raqam likhein aur purane ko khaali karein. Save karein. Purane mahine ki slip par adaigi na aaye, naye mahine ki slip par aaye, us din ki tareekh ke saath.
- [ ] Baqi se zyada raqam likhein. Paigham aaye ke yeh advance mein jayegi. **Wapas** par kuch save na ho.
- [ ] **Advance mein likhein** dabayein. Rozana hisaab mein do lines hon: Tankhwah aur Advance. **Advance baqi** us raqam se barh jaye.
- [ ] Tankhwah wali line par undo karein. Mahina phir baqi ho jaye. Advance wali line alag se undo karni hai.
- [ ] **Bank** chunein, **Reference** aur **Kis ne liya** likhein. Hisaab register mein yeh aur mahine nazar aayen.
- [ ] Advance wale mazdoor par **Tankhwah** mein **Advance katein** dabayein. **Mazdoor ko dena hai** ab slip ke baqi ke barabar ho. Mazdoor ki screen par us raqam ko badal bhi sakte hain.
- [ ] **Apni company** mein **Mahine ke baad tankhwah kitne din mein** likhein. Us din tak **abhi late nahin** likha aaye, us ke baad din ginay jayen.

## 5d. Mahina band

- [ ] **Hisaab > Mahina band** kholein. Pichla mahina khule. Jis ki tankhwah post nahin hui, us ka naam **Pehle yeh theek karein** mein aaye, aur band karne ka button na chale.
- [ ] Tankhwah post karein. Yeh line hat jaye aur button chal jaye.
- [ ] **Is mahine mazdooron ka hisaab** khud jor kar dekhein: pichla baqi + kamai - advance - katoti - ada kiya = baqi.
- [ ] **Band karein** dabayein. Paigham parhein aur **Wapas** dabayein. Kuch na badle. Phir dobara dabayein aur band karein.
- [ ] Us mahine ki kisi tareekh par **Aaj** aur **Rozana hisaab** kholein. Upar **Yeh mahina band hai** ho, aur naya likhne ka button na chale.
- [ ] Us mahine ki hazri par dabayein. Paigham aaye, aur kuch na badle.
- [ ] Rozana hisaab mein us mahine ki koi line undo karein. Wapsi aaj ki tareekh par likhi jaye, aur us mahine ki slip wahi rahe.
- [ ] Mazdoor ka rate badlein. Band mahine ki hazri aur slip wahi rahen.
- [ ] Band mahine ki tankhwah agle mahine ki tareekh par dein. Adaigi ho jaye, aur us mahine ki slip par **Ada kiya** mein aaye.
- [ ] **Yeh mahina dobara kholein** dabayein aur wajah likhein. Mahina khul jaye.
- [ ] **Rozana hisaab** mein Kharcha likhein, **Reference** ke saath. **Hisaab register** mein category ka filter chalayein.
