Aap ka dashboard, paanchwan khaka

Stripe, Shopify aur ChartMogul jaise admin dashboards ki tarz par: har safhe par waqt ka filter aur pichhle se muqabla, companies ke liye saved views aur filters, company ki poori timeline, slips ki qatar, investors ke liye business ka safha, audit log aur settings. Naye: plan aur paison ka khata, account rokna, account ki settings, aur kitab theek karna. Aur Subscriptions ka safha: kis ne bheji, kaunsa plan, kitne paise, kis bank se, slip ki tasveer. Ab har account ka apna Business khulasa (2a), aur nafa ya nuqsan har jagah: accounts ki list, Business, aur Jaiza par aap ka apna nafa.

Saare naam aur numbers namoona hain, asli data nahin. Banate waqt menu English mein, baqi Roman Urdu (malik ka faisla, 22 Nov 2026).
